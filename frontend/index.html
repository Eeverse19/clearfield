    How to read this map

    Color shows one score ingredient at a time; switch layers above. Hatched grey means NO DATA - that is not zero and not safe. Paler/darker steps are amounts of recorded evidence, never a danger or safety rating.

    Official priority needs all three ingredients: each is scaled 0-1 within its country as log(1+x)/log(1+country max), then multiplied (x1.0 unsurveyed, x0.25 surveyed, x0.0 cleared) by pipelines/scoring.py. If any ingredient is missing the zone is unscorable - never scored as 0. The chips at the top show how many zones in the selected country are officially ranked right now, computed from the loaded zone list. A preview order on an evidence layer is never a clearance order.

    ◆ marks the demo province. Colored dots are field finds recorded in this browser (green = saved to the database, amber = queued offline).

    Shortcuts: / search · ←→↑↓ pan (map focused) · +/− zoom · Esc cancel find mode. Every zone is also reachable from the ranking list — the map needs no mouse.
